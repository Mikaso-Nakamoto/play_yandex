## Об игре (100–1000 символов)
```text
Kittens are stuck in colorful bubbles — help the ginger cat rescue them! Aim, shoot and match three or more bubbles of the same color: they pop, and everything hanging from them falls down.

What's inside:
• over 1000 levels across eight colorful worlds, from Sunny Meadow to Sunset Valley;
• two kinds of goals: free all the kittens or pop every bubble;
• boosters: bomb, rainbow and a fireball that burns through everything in its path;
• endless mode with a leaderboard;
• daily gifts, stars and coins for completing levels;
• progress saving — log in with Yandex to continue on any device.

Cute graphics, calm music and simple one-finger or mouse controls. A perfect way to take a five-minute break or spend a cozy evening!
```

## Как играть (100–1000 символов)
```text
The level goal is shown at the start: free all the kittens or pop all the bubbles. Shots are limited — the counter is under the launcher.

Aiming: drag your finger across the screen or move the mouse — the dotted line shows the path and where the bubble will stick. Release your finger or click to shoot. Bubbles bounce off the walls.

Match three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them. Bubbles and kittens that are no longer attached fall down. Shots left at the end of a level give bonus points.

Tap the bubble in the launcher to swap it with the next one (Space or S). Boosters: buttons at the bottom of the screen or the 1, 2, 3 keys. Pause: the button in the top left corner or Esc.

Stones can't be popped — only dropped. A bomb on the field explodes when a bubble sticks next to it or a neighboring group pops. In endless mode a new row appears after several misses: don't let the bubbles reach the red line.
```
